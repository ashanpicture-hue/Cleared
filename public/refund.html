Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Our pay-after-delivery promise

You pay after your mismatch report arrives, and we send the corrected set once payment is confirmed. If the set fails our internal checklist, we refund our fee for that shipment in full.

What qualifies for a refund

  • A corrected set we delivered that still contains a mismatch our checklist should have caught.
  • A shipment you paid for that was never completed.
  • A duplicate or erroneous payment.

How to request a refund

Contact us through Contact with your shipment reference. We review refund requests within five business days and, if approved, refund through the same payment channel you used (bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa or Raast).

What is not covered

  • Delays or rejections caused by your bank, customs authority, or buyer — outside our control.
  • Documents you submitted that were incomplete or illegible.
  • Changes you requested after we completed the check.

Free orders

Your first 10 orders are free. No refund applies to free shipments because no fee is charged.
