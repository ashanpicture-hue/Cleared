Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect your name, phone number, and the documents you upload or forward (PO, LC, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and related files). We also collect extracted document data (quantities, values, weights, descriptions, dates, consignee, HS code, LC terms) and the mismatch reports we generate.

2. How we use it

We use your documents and extracted data to compare fields across documents, generate mismatch reports, produce corrected document sets, and notify you when a report is ready. Phone numbers are used for OTP login and WhatsApp or email notifications only.

3. Who sees your data

Access is limited to you and your assigned reviewer. Reviewers are documentation freelancers or staff who check flagged findings. We do not sell your documents or extracted data to third parties.

4. Storage and security

Documents and extracted fields are stored encrypted. Access is logged with an audit trail of every change. We follow a retention and delete policy: you may request deletion of your documents and account at any time.

5. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correct inaccuracies, or delete your account and documents. Contact us using the details on our Contact page.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use minimal cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. We do not run advertising or tracking cookies.
