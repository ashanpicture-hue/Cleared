Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2026

1. What Cleared does

Cleared checks your export documents — commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and LC or PO — for mismatches before you submit them to a bank. We compare quantities, values, weights, descriptions, dates and party details across every document, and a person reviews the report before it reaches you.

Cleared is a second check, not a guarantee. It does not replace your customs agent's filing or your bank's own examination.

2. Your responsibility

You remain responsible for the accuracy of the documents you submit and for any submission you make to a bank, customs authority or buyer. Cleared flags likely mismatches; you decide whether to correct and resubmit.

3. Payments

Your first 10 orders are free. After that you pay per shipment or by monthly Desk plan, after the finished set arrives. Payment is by bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa or Raast. We confirm receipt manually before releasing the corrected set.

4. Refunds

If a corrected set fails our internal checklist, we refund our fee for that shipment. See our Refund Policy for full details.

5. Data and confidentiality

Your documents are commercially sensitive. We store them encrypted, limit access to you and your assigned reviewer, and retain them only as long as needed. See our Privacy Policy.

6. Termination

We may suspend or terminate access for abuse of the free-order allowance, fraudulent documents, or violation of these terms. Paid but undelivered shipments will be completed or refunded.

7. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan. Any dispute will be resolved in the courts of Karachi.
